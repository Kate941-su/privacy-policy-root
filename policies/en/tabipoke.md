# TabiPocket Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026

Kaito Kitaya (the "Operator") sets out below how the smartphone app "TabiPocket" (Japanese: タビポケ; the "App") handles information.

## 1. Basic policy

The App handles your travel plans and booking information **on your device** (and, with the yearly plan or the lifetime purchase, also in your own iCloud). The Operator has no servers of its own and does not collect the contents of your trips, your confirmation codes, your name or similar information. No account registration is required.

## 2. Information stored on your device

To provide its features, the App stores the following on your device:

- Trip destinations, dates and titles
- Plan details (flight numbers, airports, dates and times, seats, hotel names and addresses, confirmation codes, notes, etc.)
- Screenshots, photos (including those taken with the camera) and PDFs that you attach or import, and images such as QR codes redrawn from them
- Settings (whether to send usage data), the number of free imports used, the status of your paid plan, and the identifiers of trips on which a Trip Pass was used

Except when you export or share it yourself (section 4), this information is not sent to the Operator's servers or to third parties. Deleting the App deletes this information from your device.

## 3. Reading screenshots and PDFs (import)

When importing from screenshots or PDFs, text recognition (Apple's Vision) and extraction of the contents (Apple's Foundation Models, or rules built into the App) are performed **entirely on your device**. Images and recognized text are never sent outside your device.

## 4. Exporting to a calendar

When you use "Export to Calendar" on a trip's screen, the App turns the trip's plans (titles, dates and times, places, notes, etc.) into a calendar file (.ics, or a CSV for Google Calendar) and hands it over through the iOS share sheet. You choose where it goes (a calendar app, Files, Mail, etc.); nothing is sent to the Operator. Once handed over, the file is handled under the terms and policies of the app or service you chose.

## 5. Storage in iCloud (yearly plan and lifetime purchase)

If you use the yearly plan or the lifetime purchase, the information in section 2 is stored in your own iCloud (the private database of Apple's CloudKit) and synced between devices signed in to the same Apple Account. In addition, for all users, a record of which trips a Trip Pass was used on (trip identifiers only) is stored in iCloud's key-value store. These are your own areas managed by Apple, and the Operator cannot see them.

## 6. Information collected by third parties

### 6-1. In-app purchases (Apple, RevenueCat)

Paid plans are purchased through Apple's (App Store) payment system. Payment information such as credit card numbers is managed by Apple, and the Operator never receives it.

The App uses the services of RevenueCat, Inc. to check purchase status. RevenueCat receives your purchase history (the plan purchased, transaction identifiers, purchase dates, etc.) and an anonymous identifier that the App creates for each device. Your name, email address and trip contents are not sent.

- RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

### 6-2. Usage analytics (TelemetryDeck)

To improve the App, it anonymously sends, via the services of TelemetryDeck GmbH, how often features are used (for example, saving a plan, whether an import result was corrected, showing the paywall), together with the device type and the OS and app versions. No personal information such as booking details, confirmation codes, destinations or names is sent. Any identifier that could identify you is hashed (converted into a form that cannot be reversed) on your device before it is sent.

You can stop this by turning off "Send usage data" in the App's settings.

- TelemetryDeck Privacy Policy: https://telemetrydeck.com/privacy/

## 7. Disclosure to third parties

Because the Operator does not hold your trip or booking information, it never provides such information to third parties. Collection by the third parties in section 6 is carried out under their own terms and policies.

## 8. Advertising and tracking

The App shows no ads and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## 9. Your rights

Because the Operator does not hold your trip or booking information, please use the App's own features (editing, deleting, or deleting the App) to view, correct or delete it. Data stored in iCloud can be deleted from your device's Settings (Apple Account > iCloud > Manage Storage).

## 10. Changes to this policy

The Operator may change this policy as needed. Significant changes will be announced in the App or on the website.
