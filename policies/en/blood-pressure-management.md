# Blood Pressure Diary — Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 1, 2026

This privacy policy explains how Kaito Kitaya ("the developer") handles information in the mobile app "血圧手帳 - 血圧記録とグラフ" (Blood Pressure Diary, "the App").

## 1. Summary

The App keeps your records **only on your device**. The developer does not collect, store, or transmit your personal data or your records.

## 2. Information stored on your device

To provide its features, the App stores the following information on your device:

- Blood pressure (systolic and diastolic), pulse, and the date and time of each measurement
- Your blood pressure goal
- Reminder settings (on/off and time)
- Display settings (text size, theme color, etc.)
- Whether you have purchased the Premium plan

This information is not sent to the developer's servers or to any third party. The developer cannot access it.

If you have turned on your device's backup feature (such as iCloud Backup or Google backup), the operating system may include the App's data in your device backup. Even then, the developer does not receive this data.

## 3. Information we do not collect

The App does not collect:

- Your name, email address, phone number, or other contact details (there is no account registration)
- Location, contacts, photos, camera, or microphone data
- Usage analytics, crash reports, or advertising identifiers (the App contains no analytics or advertising tools)

## 4. Notifications

Only when you turn on a reminder does the App ask for permission to send notifications. Reminders are scheduled on your device and no external server is used. You can revoke notification permission at any time in your device settings.

## 5. In-app purchases

Purchases and restores of the Premium plan are processed by Apple Inc. (App Store) or Google LLC (Google Play). They handle your payment and account information under their own privacy policies; the developer never receives it. The App only receives the result of whether a purchase was completed.

- Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy

## 6. Export and sharing

The export feature (PDF, CSV, Markdown) creates a file only when you choose to, and hands it to your device's share sheet. You choose where to share it (email, print, files, etc.), and the destination service's terms apply from then on.

## 7. Health information

Blood pressure and pulse are health information. They are stored only on your device and are never obtained by the developer. Please protect your device, for example with a screen lock.

## 8. Deleting your data

You can delete records within the App, or remove all data by deleting the App from your device. Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing for the developer to delete or disclose.

## 9. Children

The App is intended for adults. The developer does not collect personal data from anyone, including children.

## 10. Users outside Japan (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)

The developer does not process users' personal data, so there is no personal data subject to access, correction, deletion, or opt-out requests under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) or U.S. state laws such as the CCPA. The developer does not sell personal data.

## 11. Changes to this policy

Any changes will be posted on this page. If the information the App handles changes, we will notify you in the App or on this page before the change takes effect.
