# Piano Tap Support

For questions, bug reports and requests (including songs you would like to play), feel free to contact us.

## Contact

- Email: kworkshere@gmail.com

Including the following helps us look into your issue:

- Your device model
- OS version
- App version
- The song title, what happened and the steps that led to it

We can reply in English or Japanese.

## FAQ

### There is no sound / the sound is delayed
Turn up your device volume, and on iPhone also check the silent (ring/silent) switch. Bluetooth earphones and speakers tend to add delay, so we recommend the device speaker or wired earphones.

### My timing is right, but the judging feels off
Use "Timing adjustment" in Settings: tap the button 8 times in time with the sound and light, and judging is adjusted for your device.

### I don't know which key to press
Turn on "Light up the next key" in Settings, and the next key to play lights up in orange.

### How many times can I play for free?
You can play twice a day for free (this resets when your device's date changes). After that, you can play once more each time you watch an ad to the end. With a Pro subscription, there are no limits and no ads.

### Ads don't appear
Check that you are connected to the internet. If no ad is available, please try again a little later.

### New songs don't appear in the list
New songs are downloaded from the internet. Reopen the App while online, or pull down on the song list to refresh. Limited-time songs disappear from the list when their period ends.

### I bought Pro but it isn't active / How do I move Pro to a new device?
Using the same Apple ID or Google account you purchased with, tap "Restore purchases" in Settings.

### How do I cancel Pro?
"Manage subscription" in Settings opens the store's subscription management screen.

- iPhone / iPad: device "Settings" → your name → "Subscriptions"
- Android: Play Store app → profile icon → "Payments & subscriptions" → "Subscriptions"

Deleting the App does not cancel your subscription. After cancelling, you can keep using Pro until the end of the period you have paid for. Refunds are handled through Apple or Google.

### How do I delete my data?
Your best scores and settings are stored only on your device. Uninstalling the App deletes all of them.
