## FAQ

### Can I use it without a signal?
Yes. Your plans, attached screenshots and PDFs, and QR codes are all stored on your device, so you can view them in Airplane Mode.

### The details imported from a screenshot are wrong
Importing is a beta feature. Please correct the details on the review screen before saving. Items highlighted in orange are ones the App is not confident it read correctly.

### Can I move my data to a new device?
With the yearly plan or the lifetime purchase, your data is stored in iCloud on the same Apple Account, so you can see it on your new device. With the free version and the Trip Pass, data is stored only on your device, so please restore it from an iPhone backup.

### My purchase is not reflected
Please try "Restore Purchases" in Settings. An internet connection is required.

### Which trip is a Trip Pass used for?
The trip that was open when you bought it (or, if you bought it while creating a second trip, that trip).

### I want to cancel the yearly plan
Go to the iPhone Settings app > your name > Subscriptions. Cancelling does not delete the data on your device (syncing with iCloud stops).

### Can I add my plans to a calendar app?
From "Export to Calendar" on a trip's screen, you can create a calendar file (.ics) or a CSV for Google Calendar (in Japan time). Choose where to send it, such as a calendar app or Files, in the share sheet.
