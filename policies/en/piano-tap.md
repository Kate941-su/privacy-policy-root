# Piano Tap Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 14, 2026

Kaito Kitaya (the "Developer") sets out below how the smartphone app "Piano Tap" (Japanese: ピアノタップ, German: Klavier Tap; the "App") handles information.

## 1. Basic policy

The App has no account registration or sign-in. Your play records and settings are stored **only on your device** and are not sent to the Developer's servers. The App does, however, use the external services described in sections 4 and 5 to show ads and to manage Pro (a monthly subscription).

## 2. Information stored on your device

To provide its features, the App stores the following on your device:

- Your best score for each song
- The number of free plays used today and the date, and plays earned by watching ads
- Settings (guide, note names, sound, vibration, appearance, timing calibration, etc.)
- Whether you have finished the tutorial
- The song list received from the song server (section 3)
- Whether you are subscribed to Pro

This information is not sent to the Developer's servers or to third parties. The Developer cannot see it.

## 3. Song delivery

So that songs can be added or corrected without an app update, the App downloads the song list (sheet-music data) from a server provided by the Developer, for example when it starts. This request contains no information that identifies you and none of your play records. Due to the nature of network communication, however, the hosting provider of the server may keep access logs (IP address, date and time, device type, etc.) for a limited period. The Developer does not use these logs to identify users.

## 4. Advertising (Google AdMob)

If you are not subscribed to Pro, after you use up your free plays for the day you can play more by watching a rewarded ad (such as a video ad) to the end. Ads are served by Google AdMob, provided by Google LLC.

- The App requests only ads that are not based on your interests (non-personalized ads), and is set to show only ads suitable for all ages.
- Even for non-personalized ads, Google may collect device identifiers such as the advertising ID, your IP address (which may be used to estimate your approximate location), and information about ad views and interactions, in order to deliver ads, limit how often they are shown, prevent fraud and measure performance.
- In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, Switzerland and certain other regions, your consent is requested through Google's form. You can change your choice at any time from "Ad privacy settings" in the App's settings.
- You can reset the advertising ID or limit its use in your device settings.
- How Google uses information: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

## 5. Purchasing Pro (monthly subscription)

Purchasing, renewing, cancelling and restoring Pro is handled through the payment systems of Apple Inc. (App Store) or Google LLC (Google Play). Payment and account information is handled by those companies, and the Developer never receives it.

To check your subscription status, the App uses the service of RevenueCat, Inc. RevenueCat receives an anonymous user ID that the App creates automatically, your purchase history (product, purchase date, expiry date, etc.), the store's purchase information, and your device and OS type. Your name and email address are not sent.

- Apple Privacy Policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- RevenueCat Privacy Policy: https://www.revenuecat.com/privacy

## 6. Opening external pages

The share buttons on the result screen only open the post screen of X (formerly Twitter) or Reddit; the App itself sends nothing. You decide whether to post, and anything you post is handled under that service's terms. "Listen to the full piece" opens an external page such as YouTube.

## 7. Information we do not collect

The App does not collect:

- Your name, email address, phone number or other contact details
- Location (GPS), contacts, photos, camera or microphone
- Usage analytics (no analytics tools are used)

## 8. Deleting data

Data on your device is erased when you delete the App from your device. Because the Developer does not hold your personal data, there is nothing for the Developer to delete or disclose. If you would like the purchase history held by RevenueCat to be deleted, please contact us at the address in section 12.

## 9. Children

The App is not primarily directed at children under 13. Children under 13 should use the App with the consent of a parent or guardian.

## 10. For users outside Japan (GDPR, CCPA, etc.)

- Processing of information for advertising is based on your consent under section 4 (in the EEA, the UK, etc.). Processing of information for managing your subscription is necessary to perform our contract with you.
- The companies in sections 4 and 5 are located in the United States and elsewhere, so information may be processed outside Japan and your country.
- The Developer does not sell personal data.
- Requests for access, correction, deletion, etc. under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and similar laws can be made at the address in section 12.

## 11. Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we will announce it on this page. If the information handled by the App changes, we will let you know in the App or on this page before the change.
