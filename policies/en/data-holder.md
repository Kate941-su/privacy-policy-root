# Data Holder Privacy Policy

Data Holder (hereinafter referred to as "this app") respects user privacy and handles information as follows:

### 1. On-device storage
The information you enter or save in this app (vault names, field names and values, folder names, tags, data read from a passport, face photos, etc.) is stored only on your device. Field names and values (including face photos) are encrypted with a key generated on the device, and that key is protected by the device's secure storage (Android Keystore / iOS Keychain). Vault names, folder names and tags are stored on the device without encryption. This information is never sent to the developer or any third party. Uninstalling the app deletes the data on the device.

### 2. Network access
Except for cloud backup described below, this app does not connect to the internet. It uses no ads, analytics or crash reporting services.

### 3. Cloud backup (optional, Pro)
This section applies from the version that provides cloud backup. The feature works only when you turn it on.

- Backups are encrypted on your device (AES-256) with a backup password you choose, then stored in the app's own folder (appDataFolder) of your Google Drive.
- Your backup password and the encryption key are never sent to the developer or to Google. Neither the developer nor Google can read the contents of your backups.
- The developer runs no server and never receives, stores or views your backups.
- The only Google permission this app uses is creating, reading and deleting files in its own app folder (`drive.appdata`). It does not access any other files in your Google Drive.
- This app's use and transfer to any other app of information received from Google APIs will adhere to the [Google API Services User Data Policy](https://developers.google.com/terms/api-services-user-data-policy), including the Limited Use requirements.
- When you turn the feature off, you can delete the cloud backups from the app. You can also revoke the app's access in your Google Account settings.
- If you forget your backup password, the backup cannot be restored.

### 4. Reading passports (NFC)
To read the chip of a passport, the app uses the passport number, date of birth and date of expiry you enter. They are used only to open the chip and are not saved. Data read from the chip (name, nationality, date of birth, passport number, face photo, etc.) is saved, encrypted on your device as described in section 1, only if you choose to save it. The chip is checked for authenticity on the device with certificates bundled in the app, without any network access.

### 5. Manual backup files
Backup files you export from Settings are encrypted with a password you choose. You decide where to save or send the file; the developer never receives it.

### 6. Clipboard
Copying a value puts it on your device's clipboard, where other apps on the device may be able to read it. Copied values are cleared from the clipboard after 60 seconds (unless you copy something else in the meantime). On Android 13 and later, the value is hidden in the preview shown when you copy it. Due to OS limitations, the clipboard may not be cleared while the app is not running.

### 7. In-app purchase
Pro purchases are processed by Google Play or the App Store. The developer never receives your payment details.

### 8. Children
This app is not directed at children under 13.

### 9. Changes
If this policy changes, the update will be posted on this page with its date.
