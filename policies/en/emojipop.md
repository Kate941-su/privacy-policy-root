# Emoji Jar Pop Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 27, 2026

Kaito Kitaya (the "Operator") sets out below how the smartphone game "Emoji Jar Pop" (Japanese: えもじ Pop; the "App") handles information.

## 1. Basic policy

- The App requires **no account registration** and does not ask for your name, email address, phone number or similar information.
- Your game progress and scores are **stored only on your device**. They are never sent to the Operator's servers.
- To fix bugs and improve the game, the App uses Google's "Firebase Crashlytics" and "Google Analytics for Firebase" (section 3). The information sent to them does not include information that directly identifies you, such as your name.
- The App has no ads and no in-app purchases. If they are added, this policy will be updated beforehand.

## 2. Information stored only on your device

The following information is stored on your device and is not sent to the Operator.

| Information | Details |
|---|---|
| Progress | Coins, unlocked emoji, skill levels, decks, lives, achievements, daily missions, whether you finished the tutorial |
| Records | Number of games played, number of emoji cleared, statistics such as best score and best combo |
| Rankings | Scores for the on-device ranking, the deck used, the number that determines the board layout (seed) and a record of your moves |
| Settings | Volume, vibration, amount of effects, hints, skill button position |

- The rankings exist only on your device and are not sent anywhere to compare with other players.
- If you use your device's OS backup (iCloud, Google backup), this information may be included in the backup. The backup is handled under Apple's or Google's terms.
- Deleting the App deletes this information from your device. You can also delete it with "Reset Data" on the Settings screen.

## 3. Information collected by third-party services

### 3-1. Crash reports (Firebase Crashlytics)

When the App crashes, the following information is sent to "Firebase Crashlytics", provided by Google LLC, so that the cause can be investigated:

- The state of the program at the time of the crash (the error and where it occurred)
- Device model, OS version, app version, language setting, and device state such as free memory
- An identifier that Crashlytics creates for each installation of the App

### 3-2. Usage analytics (Google Analytics for Firebase)

To tune the game balance to how people play, the following information is sent to "Google Analytics for Firebase", provided by Google LLC:

- How the game is used (number of games played, deck composition, number of skill uses, scores, etc.)
- Screens viewed, app launches and time spent in the App
- Device model, OS version, app version, language setting
- Approximate region (country and area) estimated from your IP address
- An identifier that Firebase creates for each installation of the App

The App **does not use the advertising identifier (IDFA / Android advertising ID)** for analytics, and on iOS it does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites (ATT). Settings that use analytics data for advertising (Google signals, ads personalization) are turned off.

### 3-3. Choosing whether to send

- **If you are in the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK or Switzerland**: when you first open the App, we ask whether we may send this information. The information in 3-1 and 3-2 is sent only if you choose "Allow".
- **In other regions**: it is sent from the start.
- In either case, you can change this at any time with Settings → "Send usage and crash data" in the App. Turning it off also deletes unsent crash reports from your device.
- Your region is determined from your device's language and region settings.

For how Google handles information, see:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- Firebase privacy and security: https://firebase.google.com/support/privacy

### 3-4. Sharing and reviews

- **Sharing**: Only when you tap "Share" on the results screen, the App creates a score card image on your device and passes it to the app you choose (X, LINE, etc.). It is then handled under that service's terms.
- **Review requests**: The App may show the App Store or Google Play review prompt. This prompt is provided by Apple or Google, and the Operator receives nothing you write other than what is published on the store.

## 4. Disclosure to third parties

The Operator does not hold the information in section 2 and therefore does not provide it to third parties. The information in section 3 is collected and managed under each company's terms and policies. The Operator does not use it to identify individuals or for advertising.

## 5. Age

The App can be played by people of any age. The App does not ask for your age, and the Operator does not knowingly collect personally identifiable information from children under 13.

## 6. Your choices

- You can delete the information on your device with "Reset Data" on the App's Settings screen, or by deleting the App.
- You can stop sending usage and crash data with Settings → "Send usage and crash data" in the App.
- Because the Operator cannot identify individuals from the information in section 3, we may not be able to respond to individual requests to access or delete it. If you have questions, please contact us below.

## 7. Changes to this policy

The Operator may change this policy as necessary. Before adding features that change the information sent, such as online rankings, ads or in-app purchases, the Operator will update this policy and announce it in the App or on this website.
