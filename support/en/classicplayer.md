# Classical Masterpieces Player Support

Please report bugs and send requests via [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Kate941-su/classicplayer/issues).

It helps to include the following. Issues are public, so please do not include personal information.

- The app version
- Your device and OS version
- The track title and what happened

## About the recordings

All audio comes from the free mp3 files published by [CMSL Classical Masterpieces Sound Library](https://classical-sound.seesaa.net/) (by ANDO TOWA) under Creative Commons Attribution 2.1 Japan. The app does not redistribute the files; it plays them directly from CMSL's servers.

This is not an official CMSL app. Please do not contact CMSL about this app.

## FAQ

### A track won't play

- Tracks you have not downloaded need an internet connection.
- If CMSL removes or moves a recording, it can no longer be played. The catalog is updated monthly and unavailable tracks are removed; if one was in your Favorites, it is shown as "currently unavailable".
- Playback stops after three tracks in a row fail. Please check your connection.

### Can I listen offline?

Yes. Use "Download" in a track's "…" menu, or "Download this work" on a work's screen, to save it to your device; it then plays without a connection. By default, downloads happen only on Wi-Fi (you can change this on the Downloads screen in Library).

### A downloaded track disappeared

When a catalog update replaces a track with a newer recording or removes it, the downloaded file is deleted too. The new recording is not downloaded automatically, so download it again if you need it.

### When is the catalog updated?

Automatically around the 1st of every month. You can also check for updates from the app's Info tab.

### Can I transfer my Favorites?

Favorites and history are stored only on your device, so they are not transferred when you change devices or reinstall (there are no accounts).

### What does a tip unlock?

Nothing. Every track and feature is free. Tips are simply a way to support development; after a tip, a "Supporter" badge is shown. The badge is stored only on the device and disappears if you reinstall (there is nothing to restore).

### Are there ads or data collection?

No. See the [Privacy Policy](/classicplayer/en).
