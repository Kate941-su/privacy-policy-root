# No Dopamine — Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 30, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how Kaito Kitaya ("we", "us") handles information in the mobile app "No Dopamine" (Japanese name: ドパ脱！, the "App").

## 1. Our approach

- The App **does not require an account**. We never ask for your name, email address, or phone number.
- Everything you record in the App is **stored only on your device** and is never sent to our servers. We do not receive or have access to this data.
- The App itself contains no analytics or crash-reporting tools.

## 2. Information stored only on your device

The following stays on your device and is not sent anywhere.

| Information | Details |
|---|---|
| Records | When each streak started and ended, and the ranks you reached |
| Urge logs | Date and time, intensity, mood, place, triggers, alternative actions you took, notes |
| Settings | Habits you want to quit, your reasons, goal, notification times, appearance, etc. |
| App lock PIN | We do not store the digits themselves. A salted hash is stored in the device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore-backed storage) |

- **Biometrics (Face ID, fingerprint, etc.)** are handled by the operating system. The App never accesses or stores your face or fingerprint data.
- **Notifications** are scheduled locally on your device; nothing is pushed from a server. Notification text never reveals what habit you are working on.
- **Backups (export)** are created only when you request one, and are saved or sent only where you choose. They do not include your PIN or purchase status.
- Deleting the App, or using "Delete all data" in Settings, removes this information from your device.

## 3. Information collected by third parties

### 3-1. Ads (Google AdMob)

The free version of the App uses Google AdMob, an advertising service provided by Google LLC. To deliver ads, Google may collect information from your device such as:

- Device identifiers (such as the advertising ID), IP address, and device and app information (OS, app version, etc.)
- Interactions with ads (views, taps), and approximate location inferred from your IP address
- Diagnostic information used to investigate problems

The App requests **non-personalized ads only** (ads are not based on your interests). On iOS, the App does not ask for permission to track you across other companies' apps and websites (ATT). We also block ads in categories such as sexual content, dating, gambling, and alcohol.

In the European Economic Area (EEA), the UK, and other regions where required by law, the App asks for your consent regarding ads. You can change your choice at any time in Settings > Ad consent settings.

**If you purchase Premium, the ad components are not loaded and Google AdMob does not collect information.**

For how Google handles information, see:

- Google Privacy Policy: https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use its services: https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

### 3-2. In-app purchases (Apple and Google)

Premium is purchased through Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). Your payment details (such as card numbers) are handled by Apple or Google and are never received by us. The App only receives whether your purchase is complete, and stores that on your device.

## 4. Sharing with third parties

We do not hold your information, so we do not share it with anyone. Collection by the third parties in Section 3 is governed by their own terms and policies.

## 5. Age requirement

The App is intended for people aged 18 and over.

## 6. Your rights

Because we do not hold your records, please use the App's own features (editing records, Delete all data, or deleting the App) to view, correct, or delete them. You can manage ad-related information through the Google pages above and your device settings (for example, resetting or deleting your advertising ID).

For people in the EEA and the UK, ad-related processing is based on your consent, which you can withdraw at any time.

## 7. Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy when necessary. For important changes, we will notify you in the App or on our website.
