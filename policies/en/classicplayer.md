# Classical Masterpieces Player Privacy Policy

Effective date: November 20, 2026 / Revised: November 21, 2026 (added the producer's former audio servers and downloads for offline listening)

Kaito Kitaya (the "Developer") sets out below how the smartphone app "Classical Masterpieces Player" (Japanese: クラシック名曲プレイヤー; the "App") handles information.

## 1. Basic policy

The App does not collect personal information or usage data. There is no account, and the App contains no advertising, analytics or crash-reporting components.

## 2. Information stored only on your device

The following information is stored only on your device to provide the App's features. It is never sent to the Developer or any third party, and it is deleted when you remove the App.

- Tracks you add to Favorites, and when you added them
- Recently played tracks (up to 100)
- Recent searches (up to 10)
- The playback queue and position
- Audio files you download for offline listening, and the download setting (whether to download only on Wi-Fi)
- The track catalog and composer portraits (downloaded as described in 3-1)
- Whether to show the "Supporter" badge (if you bought a tip)

## 3. Network connections the App makes

The App connects to only two places. In neither case does the App add information that identifies you.

### 3-1. Catalog download

The track list (a JSON file) and the composer portraits (images from Wikimedia Commons, edited and hosted by the Developer) are downloaded from GitHub Pages (kate941-su.github.io). The App never connects to Wikimedia directly.

### 3-2. Playback and downloads

When you play a track, the audio file is streamed directly from [CMSL Classical Masterpieces Sound Library](https://classical-sound.seesaa.net/) (classical-sound.up.seesaa.net). When you download a track, the same audio file is fetched from the same server.

Some tracks published around 2011–2012 are played from the producer's former servers (classical-music.aki.gs, andotowa.sakura.ne.jp, classical-music.sakura.ne.jp). These servers do not support encrypted connections (HTTPS), so this traffic is not encrypted and others on the same network may be able to see which track is playing.

### 3-3. Information the servers receive

As with any internet connection, these servers receive your IP address and similar technical information, which they handle under their own policies. The Developer does not receive this information.

- GitHub Privacy Statement: https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement
- Seesaa: https://www.seesaa.co.jp/
- Former servers: the policies of their hosting providers

## 4. In-app purchases (tips)

Optional tips are processed by Apple (App Store) or Google (Google Play). The Developer does not receive your name, contact details or payment information. Buying a tip does not unlock any features or tracks.

## 5. Advertising and tracking

The App shows no ads and does not track you across other companies' apps or websites.

## 6. Children

Because the App collects no personal information, no personal information is sent when children use it.

## 7. Changes to this policy

If the App's network connections or stored information change, this page will be updated with the revision date.

## 8. Contact

For questions about this policy, please use [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/Kate941-su/classicplayer/issues) (it is public, so please do not include personal information).
