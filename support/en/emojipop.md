For questions, bug reports and requests, feel free to contact us.

## FAQ

### Can I play without a connection?

Yes. Everything in the game works offline.

### What happens to my data when I change phones?

Your data is stored only on your device, not on our servers. If you set up your new device from an iCloud backup (iPhone) or a Google backup (Android), your data may be carried over. It cannot be transferred between iPhone and Android.

### I ran out of lives

One life comes back every 10 minutes (up to 5). You can also spend 100 coins to get 5 lives right away.

### How do I get new emoji?

Unlock them with coins in the Shop, or earn them by completing achievements (such as exploding lots of 💣). The Emoji Book shows how to get each emoji.

### How do I level up a skill?

Choose an emoji in the Emoji Book and tap Level Up. You need coins and a number of games played with that emoji in your deck. Leveling up only strengthens the leader skill's effect; it does not change score multipliers.

### Can I compete with other players in the rankings?

The rankings are currently on your device only. Online rankings are being considered for a future update.

### The effects are too bright or the sound is too loud

In Settings you can change the sound effect and music volume, vibration, and "Reduce Effects".

### I want to start over

Use Settings → Reset Data. Deleted data cannot be restored.
